SINCE 2013

Built by two importers who got tired of guessing.

KJ Motors started as a two-person import desk moving a handful of Japanese sports cars a year. Twelve years later we run a 109-car showroom spanning sixteen Japanese and German marques — still inspected the same way, car by car.

OUR STORY

From one garage to a full showroom

KJ Motors was founded by Kashif John and Arnold Dean, two friends with a shared passion for cars. They began their journey with a small garage in 2014 and just five vehicles, focusing on quality, honesty, and excellent customer service.

Today, KJ Motors has grown into a trusted car showroom with 100+ premium vehicles, offering luxury, sports, SUVs, and electric cars. Despite the growth, the founders remain committed to delivering the best experience and helping every customer find their perfect car.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Three commitments that don't move

Verified history

Full odometer, auction-grade and title history disclosed on every listing — no exceptions.

Inspected in-house

Our own technicians recondition every import before it's priced, not a subcontractor.

No pressure floor

Sales staff are salaried, not commissioned on price — we'd rather you come back in three years.
