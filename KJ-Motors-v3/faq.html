COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out directly.

Are all your cars imported, or do you carry domestic stock too?+

Every vehicle on our floor is a Japanese or German import, sourced directly through our buying network in Osaka and Munich/Stuttgart. We don't mix in domestic-market trade-ins.

Do imported cars come with a warranty?+

Yes. Every vehicle includes a 90-day mechanical warranty as standard, with 12 or 24-month extended cover available at purchase.

Can I get financing through KJ Motors?+

Our in-house finance desk works with several lenders and can usually confirm approval within a business day. Use the finance calculator for an estimate first.

What's included in your pre-purchase inspection?+

A 140-point mechanical, electrical and cosmetic inspection, including compression testing, odometer verification and a title history check.

Do you accept trade-ins?+

Yes, we appraise trade-ins on-site the same day and can apply the value directly to your down payment.

Can you ship a car outside the metro area?+

We arrange enclosed nationwide transport for buyers who can't collect in person — ask your sales contact for a quote.

Is right-hand drive available on JDM imports?+

Some JDM models are available in right-hand drive; check the individual listing or ask us to source a specific configuration.

How often is the inventory updated?+

New arrivals are added weekly. The featured section on our homepage reflects the latest picks from the buying team.
